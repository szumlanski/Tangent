# HIG notes for small web apps (snapshot 2026-10)

- Touch targets 44 x 44 on phones, 28 on desktop (accessibility, buttons)
- Body text 17px on phones; large title 34px; nothing under 11px (typography). Inputs 16px+ to stop iOS zoom.
- Contrast 4.5:1 for text up to 17px, 3:1 for larger or bold (accessibility, color)
- One or two prominent buttons per view (buttons).
- Modals are bottom sheets with a grabber, close by Done or tapping outside (sheets, modality)
- Grouped inset lists for details, value on the trailing side (lists-and-tables)
- Sentence case labels, no all caps (writing)
- Motion brief and purposeful, honor Reduce Motion (motion)
- One icon set, labeled for screen readers (icons)
- Safe areas on bars and sheets (layout)
