Privacy policy

Last updated October 18, 2026

Tangent is a free news reader. This page explains what we collect, why, and what you can do about it. The short version: we keep only what the app needs to work, we show no ads, and we never sell or share your data.

What we collect

  • Account details: your email address and, if you give it, your first name. If you sign in with Google, we receive your Google account ID, email address and name from Google. Nothing else from your Google account.
  • Password: stored only as a one-way bcrypt hash. We cannot read it.
  • Your setup: your shelves, the sources on them, sources you ask us to add, and settings such as your teams and stock tickers.
  • Your feedback: thumbs up, thumbs down and hidden stories, with the time you made them.
  • Sign-in records: one record per signed-in device with its browser name and last-used time, so you can sign out everywhere.
  • Abuse protection: counts of recent sign-in attempts per network address and per email, kept for up to an hour, to stop password guessing.

If you use Tangent without an account, your votes and hidden stories stay in your browser and are not sent to us.

What we do not collect

No advertising, no analytics trackers, no fingerprinting, no reading history beyond the votes and hides you choose to make. We do not record which articles you open.

Cookies

Tangent sets three small cookies, all first-party: tg_sid keeps you signed in (signed and unreadable by scripts), tg_in tells the page you are signed in so it can open straight to your feed, and tg_guest remembers that you chose to look around without an account. Your browser's local storage keeps the last stories loaded so the app opens fast and works offline.

Who processes data for us

  • Cloudflare hosts the site and runs sign-in.
  • Supabase hosts the database.
  • Google, only if you choose Continue with Google.
  • Anthropic writes summaries of public news articles. No personal data is ever sent to it.

Stories open from the publishers' own sites, and videos play through YouTube's privacy-enhanced player. Those sites have their own policies.

Keeping and deleting data

We keep your account data while your account is open. Choose Delete account in the account menu and your email, name, password and Google link are erased immediately and every device is signed out. Remaining records (shelves and votes, no longer tied to anything that identifies you) are purged in a later cleanup.

Your choices

You can see and change your shelves, sources and settings in the app at any time, and delete your account whenever you like. People in regions with data-protection laws (such as the EU, UK and California) have the right to access, correct, export and delete their data; deleting your account covers deletion, and the rest can be requested through the same channel used to reach us.

Children

Tangent is not meant for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their data.

Changes

If this policy changes in a way that matters, we will say so in the app before the change takes effect.
