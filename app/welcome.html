How it works

Set it up once. Read it every day.

  1. Pick your shelves

    Start with ready-made topics like U.S. news, World, Business, Tech and AI, Science or Sports. Each one comes stocked with vetted sources.

  2. Add anything you read

    Paste a news site, a personal blog or a YouTube channel. Tangent checks it can read it for free and adds it to your shelf.

  3. Flip, read, teach it

    Swipe through pages on your phone or scan the grid on a computer. Thumbs up or down, and Tangent leans your feed your way.

Questions

Frequently asked

Is Tangent really free?

Yes. There are no ads, no subscriptions and no paid tier. Tangent runs on free services and only uses AI where it saves you real reading time.

How do you keep paywalled articles out?

Each article is checked when it is fetched: the publisher's own free or paid markup, known paywall systems, and whether the full text is actually readable. Locked articles are dropped. A few local papers that lock most stories can be followed as headline-only cards marked Subscriber, which open on the paper's site.

Can I add any website or YouTube channel?

Yes. Paste a site address, a feed or a YouTube channel into a shelf. If Tangent can read it without a paywall, it joins your shelf and its stories show up within about 15 minutes.

Do I need an account?

No. You can look around without one. An account saves your shelves, sources, votes and hidden stories, and keeps them in sync between your phone and computer.

Someone shared a story with me. Do I need an account?

No. Tap Read it in Tangent and the story opens right away. If you like how Tangent reads, an account is free and takes about a minute.

What is a persona?

A separate set of shelves, votes and alerts under your one login, with its own name and picture. Make one for work and one for home, or one per interest, and switch between them from the person button at the top of the app.

Can I get notifications?

Yes, on any phone or computer that supports web notifications, with or without an account. Without one you get big-story alerts; with one you can add your own words, shelves and sources. On iPhone, add Tangent to your Home Screen first.

Does it work on my phone?

Yes. On iPhone, open tangentreads.com in Safari, tap Share, then Add to Home Screen. On Android, open it in Chrome and tap Install app. It opens full screen like any other app.

What do you do with my data?

We keep your email, name, shelves and votes so the app works, and nothing else. No ads, no trackers, no selling data. You can delete your account at any time. Read the privacy policy.
