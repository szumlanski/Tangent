Help and FAQ

Answers to the questions people ask most.

The basics

What is Tangent?

A free news reader. You choose the outlets, blogs and YouTube channels you want, and Tangent gathers them into one magazine you can flip through on your phone or scan as a grid on a computer. It groups the same story from different outlets into one card and leaves out paywalled articles.

Is it free?

Yes. No ads, no subscriptions and no paid tier.

Do I need an account?

No. You can look around with the starter shelves. An account lets you choose your own shelves and sources, and keeps your votes and hidden stories in sync across devices.

How often is the news updated?

Tangent checks every source every 15 minutes. Pull the app back to the front and it picks up anything new.

How do I install it on my phone?

On iPhone or iPad, open tangentreads.com in Safari, tap Share, then Add to Home Screen. On Android, open it in Chrome and tap Install app (or Add to Home screen) in the menu. It then opens full screen and works offline with the last stories it loaded.

Reading

How do I move around?

On a phone, swipe up to turn the page and down to go back. Tap a story to read it, and swipe down or tap the arrow to close it. On a computer, scroll the grid. Tap the Tangent name, or the tab you are already on, to jump back to the top.

What does "one card per story" mean?

When several outlets cover the same event, Tangent shows one card for it. Open the story to see the other outlets under "Also covered by", and tap any of them to read their version.

Who writes the summaries?

Stories covered by two or more outlets get a short summary written by Claude, an AI model from Anthropic, using the text of those articles. Summaries can miss nuance, so read the original for anything important. Stories from a single outlet show the outlet's own text with no AI summary.

Why does an article show only part of the text?

Some sites make their pages hard to read outside a browser. When Tangent cannot get the full text, it shows what it has. Tap Original to read it on the outlet's site.

What are the All, News, Video and Blogs buttons?

They filter the current shelf by kind of source. Video shows only YouTube channels, Blogs only blogs and newsletters.

Paywalls

How do you keep paywalled articles out?

Every article is checked when it is fetched. Tangent reads the publisher's own free or paid markup, looks for known paywall systems, and checks that the full text is really there. Anything locked is dropped and never shown.

What is a Subscriber card?

A few local papers lock nearly every story. If you follow one, Tangent shows its headlines with a lock and the word Subscriber, and the story opens on the paper's own site. That way subscribers do not lose their local news, and nobody else hits a wall by surprise.

I found a paywalled article. What now?

Hide it with the crossed-out eye. Tangent rechecks sources often, and a source that starts locking most of its stories is switched off.

Shelves and sources

What is a shelf?

A shelf is one of your topics, like World, Tech or your hometown. Each shelf is a set of sources, and it shows as a tab across the top. "For you" mixes all your shelves together.

How do I add, rename or reorder shelves?

Open the menu (your picture at the top right) and choose Shelves. Shelves belong to the persona you are reading as. From there you can add a suggested topic, start a shelf from any phrase, rename, reorder and remove shelves.

Can I add any website, blog or YouTube channel?

Yes. Open a shelf in Shelves and paste a site address, a feed address or a YouTube channel. Tangent checks that it can read the source for free, then adds it. New stories usually appear within 15 minutes. Sources that are fully paywalled or have not posted in months are turned away with a reason.

How does Tangent find new sources?

In the background, Tangent looks for outlets that regularly cover your shelves' topics, checks them for paywalls and freshness, and suggests the good ones on that shelf. Nothing is added until you say so.

Teaching Tangent what you like

What do thumbs up and thumbs down do?

Thumbs up means more like this, thumbs down means fewer. Tangent learns from the source, the shelf, the kind of source and the words in the headline. Tap the same thumb again to take the vote back.

Will votes hide news I need?

No. Votes move a story by hours, not days. A few thumbs down on a source make its stories rank as if they were a few hours older, so fresh news still reaches you.

How do I hide a story?

Tap the crossed-out eye, on the card or inside the story. The whole story disappears, including other outlets' versions of it. Tap Undo in the message that appears if you change your mind. Hiding also counts as a mild "fewer like this".

Personas

What is a persona?

A persona is a separate reading profile under your one login: its own name, picture, shelves, sources, votes, hidden stories and alerts. Make one for work and one for home, or one per interest, so each front page stays focused.

How do I make one, or switch?

Tap your picture at the top right. Your personas are at the top of the menu: tap one to switch, or tap Add to make a new one. A new persona starts by picking its shelves. Each device remembers which persona it was last reading as.

How do I add a picture?

Open the menu, choose Name and picture, then Choose a picture. Tangent shrinks it to a small square on your device before saving. You can use an emoji and a color instead.

Do personas share anything?

They share your login, password, notification devices and daily alert limit. Everything about what you read is separate: shelves, sources, thumbs up and down, hidden stories and alerts.

How many can I have, and what happens if I remove one?

Up to 8. Removing a persona removes its shelves and alerts too. You always keep at least one.

Alerts and notifications

Do I need an account for notifications?

No. Anyone can turn on big-story alerts from the menu, then Alerts. An account adds alerts for your own words, shelves and sources, and keeps them per persona.

How do I turn on notifications?

Open the account menu, choose Alerts, then Turn on notifications. Do this on each phone or computer where you want them. On iPhone and iPad, add Tangent to your Home Screen first (Share, then Add to Home Screen) and open it from there; Apple only allows notifications for apps on the Home Screen.

What is a big story alert?

When four or more different outlets cover the same story within a day, and it touches one of your shelves, Tangent sends one notification for it. It is on by default once notifications are on.

What alerts can I set up?

A word or phrase (any headline or teaser that mentions it, such as "hurricane" or "FPL rate case"), new stories on one of your shelves (at most once an hour), and every new story from a particular source. Turn any of them off with its switch, or remove it.

Will I get flooded?

No. Each story notifies you once, even if several of your alerts match it. You choose the most alerts per day (10 unless you change it), and quiet hours (10 pm to 7 am unless you change it) hold everything overnight. Tangent checks for new stories every 15 minutes.

How do I turn notifications off?

Open Alerts and choose Turn off here, or turn off notifications for Tangent in your phone or browser settings.

Your account

Google or email and password?

Either one. If you use both with the same email address, they open the same account.

I forgot my password.

Email password reset is not available yet. If your account uses a Gmail or Google Workspace address, choose Continue with Google on the sign-in screen, then set a new password from the account menu.

How do I sign out of every device?

Open the account menu and choose Sign out everywhere. Every phone and computer signed in to your account is signed out.

How do I delete my account?

Open the account menu and choose Delete account. Your email, name, password and Google link are erased right away, and you are signed out everywhere.

What data do you keep?

Your email, name, a scrambled (hashed) password, your shelves and sources, and your votes and hidden stories. Two cookies keep you signed in. No ads, no trackers and no selling data. See the privacy policy.

Stocks and sports

How current are the stock quotes?

Quotes are delayed about 15 minutes and refresh every minute while the Stocks tab is open. Drag across a chart to see the price at any point.

Where do scores come from?

Scores, schedules and rankings come from ESPN's public data and refresh every minute while the Sports tab is open. Only leagues in season are shown.
